Privacy Policy

Fruit Codes Helper  ·  Last updated: October 11, 2026

Fruit Codes Helper is a fan-made companion app for the Roblox game Blox Fruits. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Roblox Corporation or the developers of Blox Fruits.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. The app does not require you to create an account or sign in.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app saves a small amount of data locally on your device using Android's standard storage. This data never leaves your device (except as described under Advertising below) and includes:

  • Cached game data (codes, fruits, accessories, guides) to allow offline use
  • Timestamps of the last data refresh
  • Your app theme preference (Light, Dark, or System)
  • Your fruit roll collection (which fruits you have discovered in the roll simulator)

You can clear all locally cached data at any time from Settings → Data & Cache → Clear cached data.

Network Requests

The app fetches game data (codes, fruits, accessories, and guides) from a public GitHub repository maintained by the developer. These requests contain no personal information — only a standard HTTPS request to retrieve JSON files.

Advertising

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier and other information to show personalised ads based on your interests.

Before serving personalised ads, the app presents a consent form powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change your ad preferences at any time from Settings → Privacy Options.

For more information on how Google uses data for advertising, see Google's Advertising Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Fruit Codes Helper is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with Roblox Corporation or the Blox Fruits development team.
